[DOMAIN]

Evidence: Title: Focuses on "IoT Botnet Lifecycle Detection." Section I: Addresses "the security of smart terminals in edge computing... including botnets"

[INTERACTION LEVEL]
Evidence: Abstract: The system provides "tailored responses to deceive potential attackers" by simulating real devices. Section I: It uses GANs to generate "interactive response data... significantly enhancing the honeypots’ ability to trap attackers"

[THREAT MODEL]
Value: IoT botnets (Scanning to Attack phases)
Evidence: Abstract: Targets the "botnet scanning and propagation phases". Section III: Classifies the lifecycle into "scanning phase, propagation phase, and attack phase"

[IRB / ETHICS COMMITTEE]
Evidence: Pages 1–16: A review of the entire manuscript confirms no mention of an "Institutional Review Board," "ethics committee," or human subject protocols

[CONSENT MODEL]
Evidence: Section I: The system is designed to "trap attackers"
 and "deceive potential attackers"
 who interact with the decoy unaware of its nature.
