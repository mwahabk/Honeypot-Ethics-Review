[DOMAIN]
Value: ICS
Evidence: Title: "HoneyICS: A High-interaction Physics-aware Honeynet for Industrial Control Systems"
. Section 1: "They represent the backbone of Critical Infrastructures... such as electric power distribution, nuclear power generation, and water supply"

[INTERACTION LEVEL]
Value: High
Evidence: Abstract: "We propose HoneyICS, a high-interaction, physics-aware, scalable, and extensible honeynet for ICSs"

[THREAT MODEL]
Value: Advanced ICS attackers
Evidence: Section 3.3 (Attacker model): "Once she has gained access, the attacker may fingerprint the target PLCs... attempt to read and write PLC memory registers and upload and execute a malicious PLC user program"

[IRB / ETHICS COMMITTEE]
Value: No
Evidence: Pages 1–10: A review of the entire paper
 reveals no mention of an "Institutional Review Board," "ethics committee," or "human subject protocol."

[CONSENT MODEL]
Value: Implicit
Evidence: Section 5.2: Data is collected from "external actors" on the Internet without notification or consent

[DATA TYPES COLLECTED]
Value: Traffic, system calls, and physical state data
Evidence: Section 3.1: It collects "logs of network interactions" and "logs of system events capturing how attackers interact with the OS"
. Section 4.2.2: Probes collect "network traffic, system calls... and any changes to the filesystem"

[PROTOCOLS & PORTS]
Value: Modbus (502), HTTP (9090), DNP3
Evidence: Section 2: "TCP/IP coordinators and workers listen and receive Modbus data via port 502"
. Section 4.2.2: "ScadaBR’s web interface is available on port 9090"
. Section 7: "our framework can also support the industrial protocol DNP3"

[DATA SENSITIVITY]
Value: High
Evidence: Section 3.3: The system allows for the capture of "PLC memory registers" and the "upload and execute a malicious PLC user program"

[DATA MINIMIZATION]
Value: Not discussed
Evidence: Section 3.1: The paper prioritizes the opposite, aiming to "collect a rich set of data about the behavior exhibited by attackers"
. There is no mention of the minimization principle.

[DATA RETENTION POLICY]
Value: Not discussed
Evidence: Section 4.2.5: "All information gathered by the probes is stored in an Elastichsearch [8] data storage for further processing."
No section defines a deletion timeline or storage limit

[DATA SHARING]
Value: Not mentioned
Evidence: No statement anywhere in the paper (Sections 1–7, Discussion, Conclusion, Acknowledgments) regarding public release of the HoneyICS implementation, source code, or the raw attack traces/malware collected during the three-month GARR deployment. The paper only references pre-existing third-party open-source tools used in the implementation (Honeyd, OpenPLC, ScadaBR).

[JURISDICTION / LEGAL]
Value: Not discussed
Evidence: Section 5: The system was exposed on an "Italian Academic network"
, but the paper contains no analysis of legal jurisdiction, GDPR, or regulatory compliance

[CONTAINMENT & HARM]
Value: Physics-aware simulation and isolation
Evidence: Section 3.1: "physics-awareness is a crucial ingredient
to realize convincing and deceiving ICS honeypots; this means
the attacker should receive consistent feed" to avoid real damage
. Section 4.2.4: Uses proxies to isolate components and "avoid lateral movements"

[DECEPTION FRAMING]
Value: Purely technical
Evidence: Section 3.1: Framing is purely functional, stating interaction levels are "essential to deceive attackers"
. Section 4.2.1: "To deceive an attacker, network requests should be routed to the proper honeypot."

[RESEARCHER RESPONSIBILITY]
Value: Implicit
Evidence: Section 7 (Discussion): "To address ethical concerns, the egress traffic was blocked on all ports... the scripts on the honeypots do not allow reaching cross-domain destinations"

[ETHICAL GAPS]
Value: Ethics-by-containment
Evidence: No explicit ethical framework or IRB discussion; Ethics addressed indirectly via safety and containment.
The paper relies on technical fixes (like egress blocking) rather than normative reasoning. (section 7)

[RELEVANCE TO PROJECT]
Value: Physics-aware Digital Twin example
Evidence: Section 1: It is a "physics-aware... honeynet"
. Section 3.1: It creates a realistic environment where attackers receive "consistent feedback from a (possibly simulated) manipulated physical process," effectively serving as a high-fidelity Digital Twin

[DEPLOYMENT]
Value: Yes (Internet-facing)
Evidence: Section 1 (Abstract): "We deployed our honeynet on the Internet and conducted experiments"
. Section 5.2: "Our honeynet was exposed to the Internet for a period of three months"
