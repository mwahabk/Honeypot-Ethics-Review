─── JUSTIFICATION TEXT (for coding) ───

[DOMAIN]
Value: IT
Evidence: Page 1: The work focuses on "Terminal honeypots," which are "a specialized type of honeypots for emulating real terminal systems"

[INTERACTION LEVEL]
Value: Medium
Evidence: HoneyGPT is a programmatic emulator that "repurposes the work of the Cowrie honeypot" for its protocol proxy".
While it uses LLMs to simulate high-interaction results, the authors explicitly baseline it against Cowrie, which they define as a "medium-interaction" system

[THREAT MODEL]
Value: Human attackers engaging in dialogue and commands
Evidence: Page 4: The system is designed for "Adversarial scenarios often involve attackers executing a series of malicious commands" and 
Abstract: "bait unauthorized entities"

[IRB / ETHICS COMMITTEE]
Value: No
Evidence: There is no mention of an "Institutional Review Board," "ethics committee," or "human subject protocols" in the entire paper.

[CONSENT MODEL]
Value: Implicit
Evidence: Page 2: The authors explicitly "deploy HoneyGPT... on the Internet for three months" to engage with live attackers, capturing "more attack behaviors and longer interactions" without any mention of obtaining consent

[DATA TYPES COLLECTED]
Value: Conversational data, commands, attacker responses
Evidence: Page 3: The Terminal Protocol Proxy "parses attackers’ commands" while the Prompt Manager "extracts the terminal output" and "maintaining the state of honeypot"

[PROTOCOLS & PORTS]
Value: SSH / Telnet
Evidence: Section IV: The system uses a "Terminal Protocol Proxy" that "repurposes the work of the Cowrie honeypot" to parse attacker commands (Cowrie typically handles ports 22/23)

[DATA SENSITIVITY]
Value: High
Evidence: Page 2: The system maintains "dynamic memory during interactions" and "long-dialogue response tasks," which involve high-fidelity logs of human-adversary interactions

[DATA MINIMIZATION]
Value: Not discussed
Evidence: Page4: No mention of the "minimization principle"; the focus is instead on "Memory Pruning" to handle context length limits for better interaction

[DATA RETENTION POLICY]
Value: Not discussed
Evidence: Page 5: "Memory Maintenance" is discussed strictly in the context of "managing the prompt’s length" rather than a formal retention or deletion policy for stored interaction logs

[DATA SHARING]
Value: Not mentioned
Evidence: Page 2: While "baseline evaluation" and P1: "field evaluation" results are discussed, there is no mention of a public link to the interaction traces or conversational datasets

[JURISDICTION / LEGAL]
Value: Not discussed
Evidence: Page 2: The paper mentions deployment "on the Internet for three months" but contains no analysis of "GDPR," "legal compliance," or jurisdictional awareness

[CONTAINMENT & HARM]
Value: Logical containment
Evidence: Page3: The framework "transforms the conventional ”requestresponse” message interaction (based on terminal protocols)
into a ”question-answer” text interaction (via ChatGPT API)"
effectively isolating the attacker's impact within the LLM's simulated environment

[DECEPTION FRAMING]
Value: Purely technical
Evidence: Page 6: Deception is central to the design, aiming to "expertly balances authenticity with the objective of enticing attackers into prolonged interactions"

[RESEARCHER RESPONSIBILITY]
Value: Minimal
Evidence: The authors discuss 
Page9: "security analysis features" and a P10: "paradigm shift" for threat intelligence, but do not provide a formal ethical responsibility statement

[ETHICAL GAPS]
Value: 1. Major ethical concerns (manipulation, consent, AI agency) not addressed
2. All attacker interaction data is transmitted to OpenAI's ChatGPT API - a third-party commercial service with no discussion of data governance, privacy implications, or terms-of-service compliance for this third-party processing"
Evidence: Page 11: The paper acknowledges technical "limitations" such as "Prompt-based Attacks" and "Overfitting," but ignores the human-centric ethical risks of AI-driven manipulation and the lack of informed consent

Section IV: "HoneyGPT transforms the conventional ”requestresponse” message interaction (based on terminal protocols)
into a ”question-answer” text interaction (via ChatGPT API)."

[RELEVANCE TO PROJECT]
Value: Critical case study for ethical risks of LLM honeypots
Evidence: Critical case study for the "technology-driven ethical lag" and the "structural ethical vacuum." While HoneyGPT solves the technical "trilemma" using LLMs to create a high-fidelity digital twin of a terminal
, it lacks the IRB oversight and ethical governance found in HoneySat
. It serves as the primary example of why a new Ethical Framework is mandatory for AI-driven systems that interact with humans in the wild without consent
.

[DEPLOYMENT]
Value: Yes (Internet-facing)
Evidence: Page 2: "We deploy HoneyGPT and Cowire, which is a programmatic honeypot, on the Internet for three months"
