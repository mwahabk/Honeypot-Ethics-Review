[DOMAIN]
Value: IT
Evidence: Section 2.1: The system is built for and tested on "Large Language Models (LLMs)" including GPT-4, Gemini-1.5-pro, and LLaMa-3.1

[INTERACTION LEVEL]
Value: Medium
Evidence: Section 1: The system is designed to mislead "tackers, prolong interaction time, and analyze behavioral
patterns". 
It maintains the "surface-level plausibility" of a real LLM to provide a realistic interaction, 
but it prevents complete system access (High-interaction) by redirecting malicious queries into a "controlled processing pipeline" and
conclusion:  "honeypot-style traps"

[THREAT MODEL]
Value: LLM Jailbreak Attackers
Evidence: Abstract: The system targets "jailbreak attacks... enabling attackers to bypass safeguards," specifically "multi-turn progressive jailbreak"

[IRB / ETHICS COMMITTEE]
Value: No
Evidence: no mention of an "Institutional Review Board," "ethics committee," or human subject protocol

[CONSENT MODEL]
Value: Implicit
Evidence: Section 3.1: The system "misleads the attacker" to " ensuring that attackers waste more resources and time" while the adversary remains unaware of the system’s dynamic conversion.

[DATA TYPES COLLECTED]
Value: Prompts, responses, and behavioral signatures

[DATA SHARING]

[JURISDICTION / LEGAL]
Value: Not discussed
Evidence: The paper does not analyze GDPR, legal compliance for deception, or jurisdictional boundaries

[CONTAINMENT & HARM]
Value: Not applicable
Evidence: No sandboxing, containerization, or VM isolation is described anywhere in the paper. The defense operates entirely at the prompt/response level via four coordinating agents (Section 3.2: Threat Interceptor, Misdirection Controller, System Harmonizer, Forensic Tracker) that intercept and misdirect LLM queries. There is no system for an attacker to escape, as the "honeypot" is a misleading conversational response, not an accessible environment.

[DECEPTION FRAMING]
Value: Purely technical
Evidence: Page 12:
Deception is described extensively as a deliberate engineering mechanism "increased interaction latency is a deliberate operation aimed at raising the costs for potential attackers," "This system-level latency constitutes an intentional defense feature" but no passage anywhere in the paper discusses the ethics of deceiving/manipulating an adversary (or of using human annotators to review harmful content).

[RESEARCHER RESPONSIBILITY]
Value: Explicit (Technical)
Evidence: Section 4.3.4: The authors frame "increased interaction latency" and misdirection as a "deliberate operation" and an "intentional defense feature" to impose costs on attackers.

[ETHICAL GAPS]
Value: Human annotators exposed to harmful content without IRB approval
Evidence: Appendix D: "A blind review by human annotators, using the same 5-point semantic harmfulness rubric, yielded high agreement with GPT-Judge evaluations." No IRB approval or annotator welfare discussion mentioned. Example harmful content includes organ trafficking prompt (Fig. 8) and presidential defamation example (Section 1).

[RELEVANCE TO PROJECT]
Value: AI-vs-AI Ethics Case Study
Evidence: This is a critical case illustrating the technology-driven ethical lag. While it uses AI agents to engage in strong human-like deception to manipulate attackers
, it lacks the IRB oversight found in HoneySat. It proves your project’s meta-finding that in high-fidelity AI honeypots, "technical safety replaces normative reasoning"

[DEPLOYMENT]
Value: Experimental / controlled
