─── JUSTIFICATION TEXT (for coding) ───

[DOMAIN]
Value: IT
Evidence: Abstract: "Windows operating systems (OS) are ubiquitous in enterprise Information Technology (IT)..."

[INTERACTION LEVEL]
Evidence: Section 2.2: The setup includes "real systems instead of low-interaction implementation... Having real systems as high-interaction honeypots maximizes the attack surface and allows full access to the underlying system"

[THREAT MODEL]
Value: Enterprise-focused attackers
Evidence: Section 2.1: "We assume that the attacker has access... by scanning the Internet... identifies the exposed services (e.g., RDP port 3389). The attacker then... attempts to authenticate"

[IRB / ETHICS COMMITTEE]
Evidence: Pages 1–13: A review of the entire paper confirms no mention of an "Institutional Review Board," "ethics committee," or "human subject protocol"

[CONSENT MODEL]
Evidence: Section 4: The system was "deployed... live in the wild for 34 days" where it received "5.79 million unsolicited connections". Attackers interact without any notification or consent mechanism

[DATA TYPES COLLECTED]
Evidence: Section 2.2: "it is essential to capture all incoming network traffic... capture the outgoing network traffic... Host logs provide fine-grained visibility". Section 2.3 mentions collecting "Sysmon logs"

[PROTOCOLS & PORTS]
Evidence: Section 2.3: "we have opened RDP (i.e. Port 3389) and SSH (i.e. Port 22)... whereas HTTPS (i.e. Port 443) is open for access to the administrator dashboard". Section 4.5 reports an "SMTP brute-force bot attack"

[DATA SENSITIVITY]
Value: High
Evidence: Section 1 (Abstract): The system captured "1.24 million login attempts" and "successfully harvested 1,250 SMTP credentials"
. Capturing plaintext credentials and real-world interaction behavior is classified as high sensitivity

[DATA MINIMIZATION]
Value: Not discussed
Evidence: Section 1 (Abstract): The paper prioritizes "comprehensive network traffic capturing" and "host-based logging". No section discusses limiting data capture or anonymization

[DATA RETENTION POLICY]
Evidence: Section 2.2: Discusses "Network Traffic and Log Backup" to "minimize the risk of losing such invaluable data", but no deletion timeline or storage policy is provided.

[DATA SHARING]
Value: Not mentioned
Evidence: Section 7 (Conclusions): While the paper provides detailed analysis of harvested credentials and geolocations, it does not provide a public link to the raw dataset or captured malware

[JURISDICTION / LEGAL]
Evidence: Section 4.1: Incoming connections were identified from the "United States and Philippines"....., Russia, and Germany
, but there is no analysis of GDPR or legal compliance for international data capture.

[CONTAINMENT & HARM]
Evidence: Section 2.3: A "Firewall & Router" is used where "outgoing connections from the HPN are blocked by default" to manage risk.
Section 2.2 notes a backup server with "not have an Internet connection"

[DECEPTION FRAMING]
Evidence: Section 2.2: Deception is used to "deceive the attackers into believing that they are navigating a real corporate network environment". Section 3 focuses on "mimicking a realistic enterprise environment"

[RESEARCHER RESPONSIBILITY]
Value: Implicit
Evidence: Section 2.2: Responsibility is framed as a technical requirement to "gather valuable intelligence on the behavior of the attacker" while preventing them from "affecting the system and network"

[ETHICAL GAPS]
Value: Structural Ethical Vacuum
Evidence: The paper follows the "ethics-by-containment" model where technical isolation replaces normative reasoning. There is no discussion of consent, legal framing, or formal ethics oversight.

[RELEVANCE TO PROJECT]
Evidence: Illustrates "ethics-by-design through isolation" by using a "Firewall & Router" and "Backup Network" to prevent harm. It provides a clear case of increasing realism through the use of "real systems" and "Windows 11 endpoints" without the proportional evolution in ethical governance (like an IRB) found in HoneySat

[DEPLOYMENT]
Value: Yes (Internet-facing)
Evidence: Section 4: "We have deployed the HoneyWin system live in the wild for 34 days from 22 August 2024 to 25 September 2024"
