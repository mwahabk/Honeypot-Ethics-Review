[DOMAIN]
Value: ICS
Evidence: Abstract:
The research focuses on "Operational Technology (OT)" and "Industrial Control Systems (ICS)" specifically within the context of "Industry 4.0" and discrete automation processes

[INTERACTION LEVEL]
Evidence: Abstract/Section 7, 2:
The honeypot replicates an "OPC UA server" and its variables over time, but the authors note that the current implementation primarily "allows the intruder to observe" and that increasing "interaction capabilities" is a goal for future

[THREAT MODEL]
Value: OPC UA-targeted attackers
Evidence: Page 3, Section 2:
The paper assumes a threat actor who has " gained access to the SCADA system" via the Internet and is attempting to interact with the "CPS located on the shop floor"

[IRB / ETHICS COMMITTEE]
Evidence: A complete review of the text confirms there is no mention of an Institutional Review Board (IRB) or ethical committee oversight regarding the study of attacker behavior .

[CONSENT MODEL]
Evidence: Section 2:
One proposed deployment (Variant 1) is to "lure the attacker into interaction" by making the honeypot "easily noticeable" (e.g., by reducing security measures) so that any interaction triggers an alarm without the attacker's knowledge or consent

[DATA TYPES COLLECTED]
Evidence: Page 4, 6:
The system captures "attacker behavior" by logging interactions with the replicated variables, which include "motor voltages," "actual yaw and pitch angles," and "angular velocities"

[PROTOCOLS & PORTS]
Evidence: While the port number is not explicitly mentioned in the body text, Figure 3 (Page 6) provides visual confirmation through a screenshot of the "opcua-client-gui" interface [38:16]. The address field in the screenshot explicitly shows the client connected to "opc.tcp://localhost:4840", where 4840 is the standard default port for the OPC UA protocol [38:16]

[DATA SENSITIVITY]
Value: High
Evidence: Page 2:	
The honeypot records "state space trajectories" and industrial protocol data related to critical industrial processes, which represent highly sensitive operational logic

[DATA MINIMIZATION]
Value: Not explicitly discussed
Evidence: Page 8:
The researchers focus on replicating as much of the state space as possible (specific "8 variables") to achieve realism, with no stated policy on filtering or limiting data collection

[DATA RETENTION POLICY]
Evidence: While the paper details how data is recorded and processed for the model, it contains no information regarding storage duration or a deletion policy .

[DATA SHARING]
Value: Dataset published
Evidence: Page 4: (link in dataset heading)
Unlike many papers in the field, the authors explicitly "publish a dataset for a cyclic industrial process" on GitHub to enable further research

[JURISDICTION / LEGAL]
Evidence: Despite being authored by researchers in Austria, the paper provides no discussion of legal compliance, GDPR, or the jurisdictional implications of deploying honeypots

[CONTAINMENT & HARM]
Evidence: Page 4:
Safety is managed by ensuring the honeypot is "isolated from the network in the production environment" and has no connection to real industrial process

[DECEPTION FRAMING]
Evidence: Page 2, 4:
Deception is framed as the technical challenge of using "self-learning models" to "authentically mimic" the fast dynamics of discrete automation, measured by the "Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)" of the generated trajectories

[RESEARCHER RESPONSIBILITY]
Value: Implicit
Evidence: Page 4:
Responsibility is framed as a technical contribution to "strengthen the system’s security" and provide a baseline for "automated threat detection and response"

[ETHICAL GAPS]
Value: Structural Ethical Vacuum
Evidence: Sections 1, 2: The paper utilizes "generative machine-learning models" to maximize deception realism for critical infrastructure while operating without formal ethical reflection, IRB oversight, or data governance policies

[RELEVANCE TO PROJECT]
Evidence: Sections 1, 2, and 6: This paper serves as a primary example of the project's technology-driven ethical lag finding, where generative models are used to create Digital Twin-like replication of CPS hardware to increase deception depth. 
The research illustrates the structural ethical vacuum identified in the meta-analysis specifically lacking the IRB review found in HoneySat despite capturing and publishing high-sensitivity industrial telemetry. It confirms the field's dominant ethics-by-containment paradigm, where technical safety is treated as a substitute for formal ethical governance

[DEPLOYMENT]
Value: Experimental / controlled
Evidence: Sections 3: The system was implemented and tested on a "demonstrator with two fans" (Quanser Aero 2) in a controlled laboratory environment rather than a live production network
